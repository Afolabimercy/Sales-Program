# Sales-Program
This project analyzes a retail sales dataset to extract meaningful insights on revenue, profit, and customer behavior. Using Python and data visualization tools, the project explores trends across product categories, regions, and pricing strategies to support data-driven decision making.
📊 Sales Analysis Project
🧾 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of a retail sales dataset aimed at uncovering key business insights and performance trends. The goal is to explore revenue patterns, profit behavior, and customer activity to support data-driven decision making.

The analysis focuses on understanding how factors such as product category, region, discount, and pricing influence overall business performance.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance across products and regions
Evaluate profit and profit margin trends
Identify high-performing and low-performing categories
Study the effect of discounts on revenue and profitability
Explore customer and sales representative contributions
Detect patterns and outliers in sales data

📊 Key Insights
Revenue varies significantly across product categories and regions
Certain price ranges generate higher profit margins than others
Discounts have a noticeable impact on profitability
Sales performance differs across sales representatives
Seasonal and monthly trends influence total sales
Outliers highlight unusual but important business cases

📈 Methodology

The dataset was cleaned and processed to ensure accuracy before analysis. Key steps included:

Handling missing or inconsistent data
Converting date fields into usable formats
Creating new features such as monthly trends
Grouping and aggregating data for insights
Building visualizations for interpretation

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was performed using Python libraries to uncover patterns and relationships within the data.

📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how raw sales data can be transformed into actionable insights using data analysis techniques. It highlights the importance of understanding customer behavior, pricing strategies, and product performance in driving business growth.
